# Pop-culture taunt signatures — Stage 0 Plagiarized Zinger gate.
# SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0
#
# Famous modern taunts are detected by keyword signature rather than stored
# verbatim, so no copyrighted dialogue ships in the application or in any pack.
# A signature fires when all of its keywords fall inside one short span of the
# volley — the phrase, not the bag of words (``corpus.KEYWORD_WINDOW_SLACK``).
# Order does not matter; distance does. That is enough to catch a quotation or a
# light paraphrase of a line everyone in the room already knows, and specific
# enough not to fire on original work that happens to reuse the same ordinary
# words elsewhere in the sentence.
#
# Keywords should be words the quotation itself is made of rather than words its
# scene is made of, and ordinary ones need a third to pin them down. Two common
# nouns are not a signature: a `handkerchief, blood` entry for a duelling line
# fired on "You dab at your lip with a handkerchief as though blood were a
# scandal", which is original, in register for both the Whitechapel and Dockside
# scenarios, and was capped at ten points and told it was borrowed. A missed
# quotation scores on its own merits; a false one accuses a player of theft.
#
# Keep the list short for the same reason the window is tight: every keyword
# widens the span the signature is allowed to cover. Three words that pin the
# quotation down beat four that merely belong to it.
#
# Format:  label | keyword, keyword, ...
# Lines beginning with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
