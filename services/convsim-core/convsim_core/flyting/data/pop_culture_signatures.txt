# Famous modern taunts are detected by keyword signature rather than stored
# verbatim, so no copyrighted dialogue ships in the application or in any pack.
# A signature fires when every one of its keywords is present in the volley,
# which is enough to catch a quotation or a light paraphrase of a line everyone
# in the room already knows, and specific enough not to fire on original work
# that happens to share one word.
#
# Format:  label | keyword, keyword, ...
# Lines beginning with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
